LegionGU by levan, based on comfyatmosphere by aloofbit (https://github.com/aloofbit/comfyatmosphere, commit bf9740c), a fog and sun rays mod for the WoW 1.12 client. Licensed GPL-3.0 (see LICENSE).
LegionGUbylevan.fx (fog and sun rays) reuses the original's shader code and formulas, so it is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0, the same as the original.
Modified in 2026: the Direct3D 9 hook was rewritten as a ReShade FX effect for the Direct3D 11 client of WoW Legion 7.3.5 (Tauri), with its own fog colour estimate, sun search, ray shaping and player controls.
LegionGUNightsbylevan.fx (night and lights) is new work in the same package, built on the depth and sky code of LegionGUbylevan.fx, and is licensed GPL-3.0 as well.
ReShade itself is not part of this package. It comes from https://reshade.me under its own licence.

wowGU.exe bundles ReshadeEffectShaderToggler (REST) 1.3.23 by 4lex4nder, MIT licence, see REST-LICENSE.txt:
https://github.com/4lex4nder/ReshadeEffectShaderToggler
ReShade itself is not bundled: wowGU downloads the official setup from reshade.me.
